🔔 Push Notifications (OneSignal)

We use OneSignal, a third-party push notification service, to send you updates, announcements, and reminders. OneSignal may collect device identifiers and notification interaction data to deliver notifications reliably. You can disable notifications anytime from your device settings.

Learn more about OneSignal's own data practices at their Privacy Policy.

🔐 Authentication & Login (Firebase / Google Sign-In)

We use Google Firebase for authentication and data storage, and Google Sign-In as an optional login method. When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (name, email) as permitted by your Google Account settings. We do not access your Google password or other Google services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data only with:

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted storage and secure authentication (Firebase Authentication), to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your data as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below. Upon request, we will delete your data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

Our App is intended for students and general users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.
